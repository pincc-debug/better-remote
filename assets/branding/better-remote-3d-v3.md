# Better Remote logo concept v3

Asset: `better-remote-3d-v3.png`. Generated with built-in imagegen. Preview only; not installed as the app icon.

## Prompt

Create an original modern 3D macOS app icon for Better Remote, a phone-to-computer remote control utility. One icon, square canvas, transparent outside the rounded-square app tile. 
Design direction: contemporary abstract product illustration with elegantly simplified dimensional geometry, a distinctive soft lavender and pale mint palette, airy soft-touch surfaces. Sophisticated and restrained, visually fresh. NOT retro skeuomorphism.
Composition: straight-on rounded-square tile in desaturated deep plum / muted violet, with only a subtle smooth tonal shift, no heavy beveled rim. At center, two freestanding ultra-simple rounded panels form a unified recognizable computer-and-phone emblem: a wider landscape display sits slightly higher and left, an upright phone overlaps at lower right. A tiny short stand integrated with the landscape panel makes it unmistakably a monitor. Panels have beautifully rounded corners and thin softly rounded depth, like sculpted matte porcelain tablets, gently tilted only a few degrees to convey depth. Dominant monitor surface is soft lavender-white; phone surface is pale celadon / mint. The front face of each panel has a subtly inset uninterrupted tonal rectangle suggesting a screen, NO shiny black screen and NO thick border. Screen and body are nearly the same hue: screen defined by minimal tonal separation and soft depth. No UI, no camera holes, no speaker, no home bar, no buttons, no reflections of real rooms. Their geometry should read as minimalist symbols rather than miniature physical electronics. The landscape screen and phone must remain recognizable at 32 pixels.
Soft upper-left studio illumination, delicate grounded contact shadows only between the two shapes, satin matte material with broad quiet highlights, no metal or glass. Strong graphic silhouette and generous balanced negative space, emblem about 68% of tile width. Tile occupies about 88% of canvas, icon outer perimeter clean and antialiased. Harmonious restrained pastel lavender/mint against muted plum; no rainbow gradients or neon. 
Critical exclusions: no black-and-white palette, no giant cropped sphere or dome, no robot face or eyes, no mouse pointer, no imitation of Grok Bot composition. No chrome, no metallic frame, no photoreal monitor hardware, no glossy skeuomorphic plastic, no 2010 icon aesthetic, no pedestal scene, no thick badge border, no perspective view of the entire tile. No text, letters, watermark, extra elements or variations. Output the actual production icon, not a mockup.
